First time here? Step by step: from the USB cable to a saved preset
  1. Connect the pedal. Plug the Cube Baby into a USB port of this computer and press CONNECT. The browser asks once for permission to use MIDI, and the Pedal line shows the pedal's name when it answers. This step needs a computer with Chrome or Edge — a phone cannot reach the pedal over USB.
  2. What you change, you hear. Every control writes to the pedal when you let go of it, so the sound moves while you play. That write is live: the bank the pedal is playing changes at once, the other two take it when you press SAVE, and nothing survives a power cycle until you save.
  3. Say which bank is playing. The badge beside A, B and C reads bank? until you tell the page which bank the pedal's own switch is on. Tap it and pick that bank: the bank you hear takes your changes live, and the other two take them when you press SAVE.
  4. Choose the preset you are editing. Press A, B or C. Everything the page shows from then on is that preset, and the badge tells you whether the one you hear is the one you are editing.
  5. Edit the blocks of the chain. The CHAIN screen draws the pedal's four blocks as boxes: AMP, MOD / DELAY, CAB / IR and VOLUME. Tap a box to reach its values, and let go of a control to write the new value to the pedal — you hear it as you make it.
  6. Keep it with SAVE. SAVE at the foot of the chain writes the preset into the pedal and reads it back to confirm it; RESET and UNDO sit beside it. An edit or a tone that is not saved lives only until the pedal is switched off.
  7. Start from a tone instead. TONES offers two ways to a preset: the LIBRARY of 92 researched tones, and GENERATE, which builds one from a description of the sound. Either way the values are shown before anything is sent — LOAD INTO THE CHAIN puts them into the preset you chose.
  8. Cabinets, and a bank of your own. BANK is where A, B and C are put together. SAVE names the preset the pedal is holding now and keeps it in this browser; the cards say which of A, B or C you are editing. EXPORT writes one saved tone to a file, EXPORT ALL the whole bank, IMPORT reads a file back, and LOAD puts it into A, B or C. The CABINET box reads the pedal's cabinet, imports a .wav — the Tone3000 catalogue is one tap away — and RESTORE puts the factory one back.

Opened with ?wire at the end of the address, the wire log prints every message the page sends and receives when something needs diagnosing. For a bug, a question, or a seat in the Android closed beta, the form at the end of this page is the way to reach us.
