Testers

      Android app · closed beta
    

    
We are opening a closed beta of the RigShade Android app, and the only thing we need from you is your email: write it below and we will send you access to the test. The app is 100% free — no subscription, nothing to pay later. In exchange we ask you for feedback: use it with your pedal and tell us what works and what you would change. A closed test needs testers who really take part and use the app, which is what Google asks of one, and your comments are what the app gets changed for.

    
Nothing else is asked of you: an address gets you in, and a line or two about how the app behaves on your phone is what we learn from.
