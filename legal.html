Privacy

This page runs in your browser. It has no account, no analytics, no cookie and no server of its own: every byte it needs is the page itself. It fetches nothing, except the one cabinet you may ask Tone3000 for — described in the next paragraph.

The Tone3000 button in the cabinet box is that exception, and it only happens if you press it: it sends you to tone3000.com to sign in to a free Tone3000 account and choose a cabinet, and you come back to this page, which then downloads that one file. The request goes to tone3000.com and to nothing else — the page's published security policy names the addresses of Tone3000 itself and refuses every other. For as long as that tab stays open the page keeps a short-lived Tone3000 session token in this tab's sessionStorage, never in localStorage, and it is gone when the tab closes or the token expires. Tone3000's own terms apply to what you do on their site, and nothing reaches the pedal until you confirm the write here.

Your presets and the bank you build stay on your computer, in this browser's localStorage and nowhere else. Clearing site data deletes them.

The page asks the browser for permission to use Web MIDI, which is what reaches the pedal over USB. That permission is the browser's, not ours. The only device the page talks to is the one you pick in the Link panel.

The tester form at the end of the control page is the one thing here that goes past that: it writes a message to the project's mailbox (rigcanvaslab@gmail.com) and hands it to your own email app, so nothing is sent until you press send there. This page itself sends nothing and stores nothing. Once you send it, the message is kept in that inbox under our own terms — and in your own sent mail, under your provider's.
